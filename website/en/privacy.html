Privacy policy

Updated: 21 November 2026

In short: Penny collects nothing about you. There is no server, no user account and no tracking.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no ads.
  • The font loads from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when you visit.
  • The star and download counts load from GitHub, so GitHub sees your IP address.
  • The site and the downloads are hosted on GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

The app

  • All data stays on your computer, in one file. Bank passwords are encrypted.
  • No telemetry and no usage reporting. The app reaches out only for what you turn on:
    • The banks and card companies you connected, during a sync.
    • A cloud AI provider, if you chose one. With no AI, or Ollama on your computer, no transaction leaves.
    • Telegram, if you connected a bot.
    • GitHub, to check for a new version. You can turn it off (PENNY_UPDATE_CHECK=0).
    • Public data for what you added: stock and currency prices from Yahoo Finance, provident fund data from data.gov.il and the home price index from the Central Bureau of Statistics. Only the symbol or series name is sent, nothing about you.
  • Automatic backups are written to the folder you chose, encrypted with your passphrase.
  • The support file is created only when you click it and sent only if you choose to send it. It has no amounts, transaction descriptions, account numbers, names or passwords.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. Report security problems as described in the security policy.
